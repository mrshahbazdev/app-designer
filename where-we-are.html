Prototype (single HTML)

Product surface

  • News / For You / Market trend — main feed modes, sub-filters (e.g. mega tabs + Voting desk → full governance).
  • Governance & voting — binding desk: sell limits, fee tiers, cap change, recovery, YES/NO patterns.
  • Market trend rail — project list + selected ballot card (Recovery, cap, sector, chain…).
  • Me drawer — profile, wallet, settings, auth gate mock.
  • Mobile mockup — standalone today-hub-mobile.html (phone-width shell, bottom nav, same tabs + governance widgets as practical).
Interactive · no backend

Brand & SEO

  • Logo — assets/trendup-mark.png (official raster) + SVG wrappers.
  • OG / Twitter / JSON-LD — patched when served over https://.
  • Theme — midnight + token variables (see design tokens doc).

Local preview

Static server (recommended)

From the mockups folder: 

npx --yes serve -l 3000 .

Then open http://localhost:3000/today-hub.html, today-hub-mobile.html, or where-we-are.html.

This page is a read-only orientation — it does not replace the prototype. Refresh the zips in mockups/ after big changes to today-hub.html or assets.
